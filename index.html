Branch of WITI San Jose

Westbridge Institute of Technology, Inc. (WITI) is a progressive technical-vocational and higher education institution in the Philippines, led by its School President, Ms. Christine P. Rosales, alongside owner and VP for Operations, Mr. Henry M. Basilan. Headquartered at its main campus in Cabuyao, Laguna, the institution is dedicated to molding disciplined and skilled professionals through competitive Senior High School tracks, College courses (such as BS Criminology), and CHED-registered technical programs like the Associate in Computer Technology specializing in CNC Programming. With multiple branches across regions—including Rizal and Iloilo—the school provides a modern, motivating environment designed to help students discover opportunities and shape a brighter future.

Our Vision & Mission

VISION Aspirations

Building a Self-Sustaining Nation

"Westbridge Institute of Technology, Inc., envisions a nation of Filipino youth committed in providing their endeavors in uplifting the Filipino lives through the skills, moral, and ethical values they have learned from the institution; a nation of well-rounded citizens with high standards of living without any need nor reason to leave the country to attain a better life."

MISSION Commitment

Empowering Future Leaders

"Westbridge Institute of Technology, Inc., conscious of the imperatives of Filipino nation building and the ideals of a total community, commits itself to provide a practical, relevant, and quality education to students who will soon become future leaders of the country and will also empower the youth with moral and ethical values for a better nation."

SENIOR HIGH SCHOOL OFFERINGS

Explore Our Tracks & Clusters

Building your future through quality technical and academic education at WITI San Jose.

ICT-SCPT

Information and Communications Technology training focused on computer systems, basic programming, and modern digital skills.

What to Expect:

  • Hands-on PC assembly, hardware troubleshooting, and networking.
  • Basic web development, software applications, and digital graphics.
  • Preparation for NC II certifications and technical IT roles.

HT

Hospitality Technology courses designed to equip students with practical skills for the service, culinary, and tourism industries.

What to Expect:

  • Practical training in food preparation, kitchen operations, and customer service.
  • Hotel and restaurant management fundamentals and front-office skills.
  • Real-world industry exposure and hospitality NC II skills training.

STEM

Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics strand tailored for students pursuing analytical and technical college degrees.

What to Expect:

  • Advanced math, calculus, chemistry, physics, and scientific research.
  • Critical thinking, data analysis, and laboratory experiments.
  • Preparation for Engineering, IT, Medical, and Science college courses.

ASSH

Arts, Social Sciences, and Humanities strand designed for future leaders, communicators, educators, and social advocates.

What to Expect:

  • Public speaking, creative writing, social research, and debate.
  • Understanding human behavior, societal issues, arts, and governance.
  • Preparation for Law, Education, Communication, Psychology, and Public Admin.

ABM

Accountancy, Business, and Management strand built for future entrepreneurs, corporate professionals, and business leaders.

What to Expect:

  • Fundamentals of accounting, business math, marketing, and economics.
  • Creating business plans, financial management, and simulated feasibility studies.
  • Preparation for Accountancy, Business Admin, Finance, and Entrepreneurship degrees.
